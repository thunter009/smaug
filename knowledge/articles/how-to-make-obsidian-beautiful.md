Walkthrough of the theme, fonts, settings and plugins from james bedford's six-year Obsidian vault.

## Key Takeaways
- Theme: Minimal by Kepano; main font iA Writer Quattro plus a mono font
- Deliberately few plugins (seven) to avoid brittleness from community plugins
- Plugins include Calendar (daily notes), Hider (hide UI chrome for a clean look), Kanban (writing tasks), Outliner (Roam-style nested bullets)
- Minimal theme settings with Flexoki colour scheme
