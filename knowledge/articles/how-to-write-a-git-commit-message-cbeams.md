Chris Beams' 2014 guide to good commit messages. The standard reference that expands on Tim Pope's note into seven rules.

## Key Takeaways
- Separate subject from body with a blank line.
- Limit subject to 50 chars, capitalize it, no trailing period.
- Use imperative mood in the subject.
- Wrap body at 72 chars; explain what and why, not how.
- Commit messages are a durable communication channel with future maintainers.
