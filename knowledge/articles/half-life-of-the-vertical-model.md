Why six vertical AI companies shipped their own models in one week (Cognition SWE 1.5, Cursor Composer 2, Decagon, Hippocratic Polaris 3, Intercom Fin Apex 1, EvenUp Piai), and whether domain post-training advantages last once the next frontier model lands.

## Key Takeaways

- Bull case: general models over-serve and under-serve verticals; post-training on proprietary evals closes the gap fast
- Intercom Apex replaced Sonnet 4.0 in Fin; one customer's resolution rate rose 68% to 75% (22% fewer unresolved)
- The moat is eval infrastructure and proprietary data, not weights
- Bear case: fine-tunes take months, frontier updates repeatedly erased earlier advantages
- Three explanations for the wave: narrative positioning, genuine technical inflection, demand-side pull (latency, cost, compliance)
