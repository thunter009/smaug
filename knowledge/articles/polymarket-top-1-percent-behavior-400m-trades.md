X article claiming a Claude analysis of 400M Polymarket trades across 2,400 filtered wallets (about $890M volume). Claims are unverified and self-reported.

## Key Takeaways

- Top 30 wallets reportedly outperform the bottom 2,370 by 11.4x on risk-adjusted PnL.
- Capital velocity: top wallets enter late (about 72 hours before resolution) and recycle capital about 47x per year versus 6x for average wallets.
- Category concentration: top wallets trade about 1.7 categories versus 8.3; claimed win-rate decay as categories grow, Kelly fraction negative beyond a few categories.
- Exit asymmetry (disposition coefficient) is described as the largest alpha source.
