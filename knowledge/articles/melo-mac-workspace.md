Native Mac workspace for capturing notes, links and screenshots with a global hotkey (Option+Space), then asking questions about them in plain language via an AI "Brain chat". Spatial boards/canvases hold notes, docs, web pages and todo lists, with voice and gesture control and a clipboard manager. Positioned as a Notion replacement.

## Key Takeaways

- One-keystroke capture plus natural-language recall from saved memory
- Customizable canvases with an AI assistant that sees full board context
- Free to start; Pro from $4.99/mo or $29.99 lifetime
