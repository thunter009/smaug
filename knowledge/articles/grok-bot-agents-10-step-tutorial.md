Tutorial for Grok Bot, a desktop app where each named bot gets its own cloud computer, logs into tools, and runs routines unattended. Core idea: delegate roles, not prompts, and draw the approval line at reversibility.

## Key Takeaways

- Name bots after job titles (Inbox Manager, Expense Manager) and write a charter: what it owns, what good looks like, where it must stop.
- Connect tools once at account level; login handoff gives the bot a session, never a pasted secret.
- Teach workflows by demonstration, then attach a schedule or trigger ("run this every week").
- Run specialist bots, group them in chats with an objective rather than a task list.
- Approval rule: finish reversible work alone (draft, file, tag); park anything external, monetary, public, or irreversible.
- Review weekly: did it run, was output right, would I miss it; prune the rest.
