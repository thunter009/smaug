## Key Takeaways

- Problem framed as "context debt": every new AI chat starts cold, so keep a persistent knowledge layer.
- Flat structure for the machine: AGENTS.md (master context: who you are, goals, rules), inbox/ (raw staging), notes/ (interlinked facts), ideas/ (original thinking), projects/ (active work).
- Passive ingest removes capture friction: scheduled Codex computer-use or browser prompts export last-24h X bookmarks and YouTube transcripts as Markdown into inbox/.
- A daily "evolution" prompt audits new files, cross-references the roadmap in AGENTS.md and updates memory.
- When structure changes, tell the agent in AGENTS.md that the map changed.
