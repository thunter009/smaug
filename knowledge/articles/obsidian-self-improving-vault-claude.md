## Key Takeaways

- Vaults fail from capture friction, no connection layer, no reason to return
- Capture must be automatic (articles, tweets, voice notes)
- Claude reads across the vault to surface connections and daily insights
- Vault context file: who I am, projects, how vault works, what I want
- Trading profit claims in the tweet are anecdotal and unverified
