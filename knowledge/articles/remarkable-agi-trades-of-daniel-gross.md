John Coogan reviews the 18 questions in Daniel Gross's January 2024 "AGI Trades" page against two years of outcomes (to March 2026).

## Key Takeaways

- Value accrued to infrastructure: Nvidia added about $3.2T market cap ($1.2T to $4.4T); revenue tripled from $60.9B to $215.9B. Microsoft returned only about 4%.
- Copper was badly mispriced ($3.75/lb to $6.61/lb record). Energy became the game: Vistra +321%, nuclear PPAs (TMI restart, Kairos, Meta 6.6 GW). Coal mixed.
- Data center bottleneck was power transformers: lead times near 3 years, 30% supply shortfall.
- US dominates AI investment ($109B in 2024, 12x China). Indian IT shed about 58k jobs in 2024-25. Entry-level tech hiring down 25%.
- GPT-4-level inference cost fell from $20 to $0.40 per million tokens (50x in 3 years); lab revenue still rises.
- Leading-edge nodes and interconnect matter; no frontier model trained on hardware older than 5nm. Taiwan tension escalating.
