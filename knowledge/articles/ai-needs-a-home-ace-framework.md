Argues AI assistants forget between sessions and need a shared, structured Obsidian-style vault. Proposes the ACE folder model (Atlas, Calendar, Efforts) as a universal mental model for human and AI knowledge management.

## Key Takeaways

- Atlas = timeless knowledge (ideas, sources, people)
- Calendar = time-based info (daily notes, plans, logs)
- Efforts = time-bound projects and areas
- Supporting folders: + for new unsorted notes, x for extras and templates
- AI should log each day into the shared vault to fix poor memory/retrieval
