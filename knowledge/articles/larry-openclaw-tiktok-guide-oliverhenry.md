Oliver Henry describes handing TikTok slideshow marketing for his iOS apps (Snugly, Liply) to "Larry", an OpenClaw agent on an old gaming PC running Ubuntu. Within 5 days posts passed 500K views (best 234K), lifting MRR to $588.

## Key Takeaways

- The agent handles the daily grind: generates 6 slideshows, writes captions, researches what works, posts on schedule.
- The human role is narrow: picks music, approves hooks, critiques images.
- Persistent identity and real tool access turn OpenClaw into an autonomous agent, not a chatbot.
