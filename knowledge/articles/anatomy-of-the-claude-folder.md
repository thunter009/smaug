Complete guide to the .claude directory: CLAUDE.md, custom commands, skills, agents, permissions and memory. Distinguishes the committed project-level folder from the machine-local ~/.claude.

## Key Takeaways

- Two folders: project (team config, committed) and home (personal, memory)
- CLAUDE.md is the instruction file Claude follows
- Commands, skills, agents and permission rules live in the folder
