Interactive map from Heatmap News and MIT (with CleanEcon) showing monthly electricity prices per kWh and average bills for major US utilities since Jan 2020.

## Key Takeaways

- Toggle between bill and price datasets; view by state, utility, congressional district, county, or zip code.
- Address lookup, monthly timeline slider with play, relative/absolute color scales, colorblind palette.
- Map images can be copied or downloaded.
