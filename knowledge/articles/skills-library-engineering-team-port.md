## Key Takeaways

- Skills live in GitHub; auto-discover scattered shadow skills
- Group by use case; required skills pushed automatically, optional role-specific
- Engineers get skills with one command in their IDE
- Feedback loop and tracking whether skills work
- Goal is to prevent agent sprawl across the team
