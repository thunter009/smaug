Guidance on pruning accumulated skills, AGENTS.md and prompts now that coding agents need less scaffolding. Argues bloated instructions hurt newer models.

## Key Takeaways

- Keep skill descriptions short and precise; too many skills get truncated and "pick me" wording misfires.
- Use progressive disclosure: root skill as minimal router to supporting docs.
- Drop recipe-style itineraries, mandatory repo-map reads, and "run tests" nags the model now does itself.
- Describe decision boundaries carefully; the model takes them seriously and may stop too early.
- Define completion up front (run, inspect, fix) to counter tentative stopping.
