Teams vary hugely in how often they have preventable emergencies. Better managers have fewer.

## Key Takeaways

- Know how hard things are: managers stay expert in the team's work, and ask "what would it take?" before demanding "TODAY".
- Set expectations and explain reasoning with each ask.
- Know what is important: strong conviction about the roadmap lets you push back on executive fire drills.
- Keep a mental model of team, company, and industry to forecast future demands (e.g. AI prototyping for design teams).
- Care about the team: emergencies are often a choice to trade team time for short-term gain.
