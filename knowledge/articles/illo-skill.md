Agent skill for original editorial illustrations, mini-comics, explainer diagrams and character cutouts. Uses reference-locked character packs (58 recurring characters across 17 looks) so a series of images keeps a consistent character.

## Key Takeaways
- Reference-locked characters keep the 10th image consistent with the first
- Built-in QA re-rolls generations to raise quality
- 30 community characters available, or build your own
- Site exposes llms.txt, agent-instructions.md, characters.json and an OpenAPI spec for agents
