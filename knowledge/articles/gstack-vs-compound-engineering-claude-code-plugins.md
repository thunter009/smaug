Compares two free, open-source Claude Code plugins that turn it into a structured environment: gstack by Garry Tan and Compound Engineering by Every. Conclusion in the tweet: use both.

## Key Takeaways

- gstack is Garry Tan's setup (40k+ stars in 12 days): a virtual team of CEO, eng manager, designer, QA lead, security officer and release engineer.
- Core gstack commands: `/office-hours`, `/plan-ceo-review`, `/plan-eng-review`, `/review`, `/qa`.
- Compound Engineering is Every's plugin; the article contrasts how the two differ and when to pick each.
