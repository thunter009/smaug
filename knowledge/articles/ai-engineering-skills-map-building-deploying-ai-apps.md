Andrew Ng expands the first top-level skill of his AI Engineering Skills Map, derived from job postings, expert interviews and surveys.

## Key Takeaways

- Six sub-skills: LLM foundations, grounding models with data, agentic systems, evaluation-driven development, production operations, ML foundations.
- AI apps differ from traditional software because output is unpredictable, so building is iterative: build, examine, decide next step.
- LLM foundations include model choice, sampling parameters, tool calling, fine-tuning and self-hosting.
