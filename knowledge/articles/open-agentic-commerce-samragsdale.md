Sam Ragsdale argues ACP and UCP (checkout inside ChatGPT and Gemini) are an incremental step, and that open protocols will reshape commerce the way the open web beat AOL's curated bundle. He traces the web's unused HTTP 402 "payment required" message and how ads (Google) replaced micropayments, then argues LLM agents are not distracted by ads, which breaks the ad model.

## Key Takeaways

- Two 1990s visions: AOL curated bundle vs open protocols (HTTP, DNS, HTML); open won, no gatekeepers
- HTTP 402 was designed for micropayments, but no digital payment method existed, so ads filled the gap
- Agents do not get distracted by adverts, so attention monetization weakens
- Agents will need agent-native payment infrastructure (his project: agentcash)
- Chollet's quote-tweet agrees and expects over 10x growth in transactions
