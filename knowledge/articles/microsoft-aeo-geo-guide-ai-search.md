Alex Groberman's X article breaks down Microsoft's retailer guide to AI search. Core claim: competition shifts from being found to being chosen. AEO makes content machine-readable so assistants and agents can find, summarize, recommend and act on it. GEO builds authority so generative engines cite it. Bookmarked via a quote tweet claiming a brand made over $50k from ChatGPT, Google and Perplexity traffic by following it.

## Key Takeaways

- AI shopping replaces ranking/clicks/visits with answers, recommendations and agent decisions
- AEO = clarity and machine-readability; GEO = credibility, reputation and citability
- SEO still matters; pair technical SEO with structured, intent-driven content and authority signals
