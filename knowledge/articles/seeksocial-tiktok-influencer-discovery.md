SeekSocial is a SaaS landing page for a searchable database of over 9.9M TikTok influencers with emails, engagement rates and other metrics. @Dinosn shared it with the claim that all public TikTok endpoints were reverse engineered, scraping 5.94 billion videos and 3.23 billion profiles in 3 weeks.

## Key Takeaways

- Product: discover TikTok creators and get contact emails plus engagement rate for brand campaigns.
- Scale claim: 9.9M influencers indexed; 5.94B videos and 3.23B profiles scraped (per tweet, unverified).
- Relevant as a data source for influencer outreach and creator lead lists; scraping legality/ToS risk applies.
