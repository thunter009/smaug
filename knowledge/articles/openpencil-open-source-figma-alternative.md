OpenPencil is an MIT-licensed, local-first design editor built in about three days by the author of figma-use. It reads and writes Figma .fig files, exposes design tools to an AI chat/agent, and supports serverless P2P collaboration in a ~7 MB app with no account.

## Key Takeaways
- Opens and saves Figma .fig files; runs locally on Windows, macOS, and web
- AI chat with full design tools; fully programmable via SDK/automation by you or your agent
- P2P collaboration with zero servers; free, MIT license
- Author claims Figma silently patched to break his figma-use MCP tool (JSX export, linting, design creation), then shipped its own MCP server
