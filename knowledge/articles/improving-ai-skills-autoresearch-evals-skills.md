Three attempts at auto-tuning AI skills. Machine-generated judges optimised the wrong things; Hamel Husain eval skills improved inputs but not judges; real gain needed reading outputs and building intuition by hand.

## Key Takeaways

- Autoresearch optimises exactly the criteria given; ungrounded criteria yield wrong improvements
- Structured synthetic inputs (dimensions then tuples) beat vibed test cases
- Judges hold comprehension; humans must read outputs and correct them
