ClearSERP is a keyword research and SERP analysis platform (Domain Scores, SERP weakness detection). Ian (@keywordian) used its Auto Research feature to screen 35,000+ candidate keywords down to 6,000+, propose 244 articles with merged near-duplicate SERPs, and build a pillar/spoke topical map with internal link suggestions in under an hour, versus an estimated 80-100 manual hours.

## Key Takeaways

- Auto Research agent mode automates keyword discovery, screening against user requirements, clustering and topical mapping
- Near-duplicate SERPs are merged into a single article
- Claimed 80-100x time saving for a narrow niche
