Jason Lemkin argues the CRM choice is now an AI infrastructure choice: pick the CRM where your sales agents live. Compares Salesforce, HubSpot and AI-native newcomers Lightfield, Monaco and Aurasell.

## Key Takeaways

- Large sales teams with a proven CRO: still Salesforce, now the hub for GTM agents (Agentforce, Qualified, Artisan)
- Smaller teams not yet deep on agents: HubSpot remains a strong product
- AI-native early-stage startups may start on Lightfield, Aurasell or Monaco
- Each deployed agent deepens lock-in: 2-3 agents is annoying to switch, 20 is functionally impossible
