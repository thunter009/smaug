Agent skill that runs a full Answer Engine Optimization audit: what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity say about your product, why, and what to fix.

## Key Takeaways

- Separates "AI doesn't know you" from "AI knows but won't recommend you"; fixes differ
- Compares engine claims (pricing, features, integrations) against codebase truth
- Audits 25 AI crawlers: visibility cost versus training opt-out only
- Maps cited sources in your category and which cite competitors
- Reports carry sample sizes and confidence intervals; ships dated report and ordered gameplan naming files
- Optional: DataForSEO MCP (~1 cent/call), Search Console, server logs, Ahrefs
