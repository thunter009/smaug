Anh Tho (Lago) describes turning years of her own hand-written posts, emails and edits into a Claude Skill that captures her writing voice. She spoke at the Write and Learn workshop hosted by swyx and MilksandMatcha. She lists ten Hacker News posts (200-777 points) as proof of her track record. She open-sources the template as `getlago/inside-lago-voice-skill`.

## Key Takeaways

- Built from her own writing with no AI in the loop. She reverse-engineered what she keeps, what she always deletes, and how she rewrites AI drafts before sending.
- The skill encodes the delta between AI drafts and what she actually sends: anti-patterns, repetition, structure and formatting.
- Emotion and the truth you need to say cannot live in a skill. The skill saves hours of editing weekly but does not replace the writer.
- The template has the same structure with empty fields for you to fill in.
