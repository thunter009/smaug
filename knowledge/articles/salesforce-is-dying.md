# Salesforce is dying and there's a way to save it

Argues AI is shifting enterprise software value from systems of record (filing cabinets like Salesforce) to systems that do the work. Cites Salesforce cutting 4,000 support jobs, its stock falling 26% and the software ETF dropping from $117 to $82.

## Key Takeaways

- Software-as-product trends toward zero; value moves to outcomes and experiences
- Outcome companies capture the labor budget rather than the software budget
- Infrastructure earns premium pricing only when scarce (NVIDIA yes, CRM no)
- Maps to Pine and Gilmore's Progression of Economic Value (1998)
