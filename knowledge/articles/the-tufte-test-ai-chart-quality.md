Encodes seven Tufte principles as an evaluation API in Truesight. A Manus agent produced a default chart, failed direct labeling and annotations, then self-revised in two prompts.

## Key Takeaways

- A precise quality standard gives agents an exact fix list that vague prompts cannot
- Encode expert judgment once as an API, every agent in the stack builds against it
- Result: direct labels, key-insight subtitle, annotation of CS peak at 37.1% in 1983
