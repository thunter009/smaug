Prompting guide for intentional frontends with GPT-5.4: tight constraints, visual references, real content, and a narrative page structure.

## Key Takeaways

- Use low or medium reasoning; define design system, then supply visual references
- Landing: full-bleed hero, two typefaces max, one accent, one job per section
- Write visual thesis, content plan and interaction thesis before building
- Apps: Linear-style restraint, cards only when they are the interaction
