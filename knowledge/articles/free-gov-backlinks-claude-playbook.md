Playbook for earning free .gov backlinks by registering the business in government supplier directories, with Claude filling the forms. Promotes Distribb (link-building SaaS).

## Key Takeaways

- Government vendor directories are free, sit on .gov domains, and link to your site; few businesses claim them.
- Steps: register on SAM.gov (free Unique Entity ID), self-certify small business, register on state vendor portal, let Claude fill profiles, use national equivalent outside US, verify links went live.
- Claim: .gov listing is a trust signal AI search reads (uncertain; vendor-authored).
