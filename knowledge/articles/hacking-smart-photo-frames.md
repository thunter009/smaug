Wes Bos walks through rooting roughly $50 Amazon smart photo frames and turning them into home display dashboards.

## Key Takeaways
- Frames have 1280x800 touch screens, WiFi, and USB power.
- They can be rooted and updated to run a modern browser.
- Result is a cheap always-on family dashboard.
