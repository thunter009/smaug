Luke Pierce (Boom Automations, 90+ custom builds) argues that for $2M-$10M revenue companies (~20 staff, no ERP, fragmented low-code tools) replacing the stack with one custom AI-native system is the highest-ROI AI move. One login, one database, AI built into workflows with full context.

## Key Takeaways

- Bolting AI onto fragmented tools fails; AI is only as useful as the data it can see
- Six-step build, sequence matters; Step 1 is mapping every workflow by department
- Mapping: list triggers/steps/done state, walk with the person who runs it, mark handoffs and duplicate entry, time manual work to size ROI
- Claims near 100% adoption on these builds; skip the legacy ERP era
