Kevin Ho argues personal health lacks monitoring, visualization and feedback loops, and pitches Otto (ottolab.com) and its BioMap treemap of biomarkers.

## Key Takeaways

- Lab "normal" ranges come from a mostly metabolically unhealthy population; fasting glucose 99 was "normal" but suboptimal, and fasting insulin is not on standard panels.
- BioMap: treemap where block size = clinical significance, color = status.
- Feedback loop: map each supplement/intervention to a target biomarker, retest in 90-180 days, diff results ("GitHub for health interventions").
- Protocol: add fasting insulin, ApoB, Lp(a), hs-CRP, thyroid, vitamin D, homocysteine; benchmark against optimal ranges; compute HOMA-IR.
