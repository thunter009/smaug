Hamel Husain argues that when AI output is hard to verify, the product design is usually at fault. If an engineer cannot easily judge outputs, end users cannot either. Better product design is upstream of evals.

## Key Takeaways

- Hard-to-eval AI output signals a product design problem, not just a measurement problem.
- Products should make verification of AI output easy for users.
- Three interactive before/after examples: an AI data agent answering business questions, a K-12 PE lesson-plan generator, and a workers' comp tool drafting 50-page medical reports.
- Design is a bottleneck in AI engineering that sits upstream of eval tooling.
