Addy Osmani co-wrote a Google whitepaper, "New SDLC Vibe Coding", on how AI reshapes the software lifecycle. The blog post pulls out the ideas he thinks matter, plus six reusable figures. Core theme: the job shifts from writing code to judging it, and lead times collapse from weeks to hours or minutes.

## Key Takeaways

- An agent is model plus harness. The paper's rough split is 10% model, 90% harness (instructions, tools/MCP, sandboxes, orchestration, hooks, observability).
- Harness changes alone move benchmarks: one team went from outside top 30 to top 5 on Terminal Bench 2.0; LangChain gained 13.7 points via prompt, tools and middleware.
- Debug the harness first when an agent misbehaves; most failures are configuration failures.
- Context engineering is the key knob inside the harness; the paper sorts agent context into six types (instructions, knowledge, memory, and more).
- Figures depict the collapse of phase lead times, praised by Gene Kim as roughly 3 orders of magnitude.
