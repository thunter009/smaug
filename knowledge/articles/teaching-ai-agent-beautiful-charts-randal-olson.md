Blog series of data visualizations built by an AI agent and critiqued against chart-quality standards inspired by Edward Tufte. Each post tells a data story with a publication-quality chart.

## Key Takeaways

- Project builds a quality harness so the agent produces better dataviz.
- Agent researches, charts and writes each article autonomously.
- Example posts: heaviest AI users are most worried about it; only the youngest workers lose ground in AI-exposed jobs; US oil dependence and the Strait of Hormuz.
