Install guide for NOOP, a free, anonymous, independent app that reads Whoop data without a subscription. It is not on the App Store or TestFlight, so it needs sideloading.

## Key Takeaways
- Easiest path: AltStore or SideStore, installing the `NOOP-vX-ios.ipa` from github.com/NoopApp/noop/releases
- Free Apple ID signing expires after 7 days; AltStore/SideStore refresh in the background
- Sideloaded with a free ID, Apple Health, Live Activity and widgets may be limited; pairing, live HR, recovery, strain, sleep and history still work
- Most complete option: build from source with XcodeGen and the NOOPiOS scheme in Xcode, which grants entitlements via your own Apple ID
