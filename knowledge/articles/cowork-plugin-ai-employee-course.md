## Key Takeaways

- A Cowork plugin is just a folder: `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` (identity), `skills/*/SKILL.md` (step-by-step workflow), `commands/` (slash triggers), `references/` (templates, benchmarks), plus global and folder instructions.
- SKILL.md is the core file; write aggressive trigger phrases and explicit "do not use for" cases in its description.
- Phase 1 (about 30 min): research the real professional workflow for the role, then add your own shortcuts, quality checks and failure modes.
- Phase 2 (about 60 min): write SKILL.md with overview, numbered steps, output format, rules, edge cases and a quality checklist.
- Best plugins encode your specific expertise, not generic best practice.
