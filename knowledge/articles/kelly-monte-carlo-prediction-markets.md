Explains using the Kelly Criterion for bet sizing in prediction markets, with Monte Carlo simulation to test it. Covers Kelly's original formula and Thorp's generalized form using expected return, risk-free rate and volatility.

## Key Takeaways

- Kelly maximizes long-run wealth but does not guarantee success in any single run
- Thorp's form needs robust estimates of return and volatility, which depend on the lookback window
- Article applies Thorp-Kelly to prediction market contracts in six strategies, simulated via Monte Carlo
