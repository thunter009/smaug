X article describing a pipeline for turning TV series episodes into Anki flashcards (504 cards from one series). Claims language apps teach words in a vacuum, while contextual and emotional input from real scenes makes vocabulary stick.

## Key Takeaways

- Language learning is about input, context, and repetition at the right intervals, not app streaks
- Pipeline: get an episode transcript, NotebookLM extracts ~50 useful words/expressions (phrasal verbs, idioms, slang, B2-C1 vocabulary), Claude formats Anki-ready cards, Anki drills them via spaced repetition
- NotebookLM chosen because it is source-grounded: example sentences come from the transcript, not invented
- Each episode yields roughly 50-100 learnable items; goal is watching full episodes without pausing after about one season
