Open Standard is a stablecoin initiative announced by Patrick Collison with partners Visa, Mastercard, Coinbase, Cloudflare, Google and others. Its token, Open USD (OUSD), is pitched as the first stablecoin designed as open infrastructure, giving businesses the economics, governance and reliability they need to move money. Only the landing-page metadata was captured; detailed terms were not.

## Key Takeaways

- OUSD is positioned as shared infrastructure, not a single-issuer product.
- Broad payments and tech partner list (card networks, exchange, CDN, Google).
- Emphasis on economics, governance and reliability for business use.
