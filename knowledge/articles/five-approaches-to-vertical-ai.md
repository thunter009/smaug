Framework of five entry points for vertical AI founders in 2026. Vertical AI no longer means only selling SaaS to underdigitized industries. Buckets are wedges, and the best companies move between them.

## Key Takeaways

- AI-native services: compete inside the industry (AI law firm, AI accountant) and target labor spend, not IT spend. Larger TAM and near-software margins, unlike 2010-2020 tech-enabled services.
- Vertical AI platforms of record: command center for agents/copilots in one industry. Voice is the strongest entry point for non-desk workers, and capturing voice data first compounds.
- Workflow automation: high-volume paperwork in healthcare and insurance. Works now, but standalone window may close as horizontal tools improve. Use as a wedge.
- Biz in a box + AI: SMBs will not pay high ACV for software. Monetize by owning a back-office task or taking revenue share, and enable new operators to launch.
- Custom automation platforms: Palantir-style forward-deployed AI for Fortune 500s. Start n-of-1, build reusable components, scale to peers.
