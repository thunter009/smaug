Roan argues that combining many weak, independent signals beats hunting for one strong signal. He cites the Fundamental Law of Active Management and lays out an 11-step weighting procedure, then applies it to prediction markets. The research numbers he cites are unverified here.

## Key Takeaways

- Fundamental Law of Active Management: IR = IC × √N, so many weak independent signals can beat one strong signal
- Typical institutional signal information coefficients cited at 0.05 to 0.15
- Five signal families: momentum, mean reversion, volatility, factor, microstructure
- 11-step procedure: demean and normalize returns, remove cross-sectional common effects, weight signals by forward expected return and residuals
- Prediction-market application: combine implied-probability signals, size with empirical Kelly, execute with VWAP, watch VPIN
- Author claims 400M Polymarket trades show markets priced 5-15% resolve YES 4-9% of the time (unverified)
