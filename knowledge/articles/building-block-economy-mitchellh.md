Mitchell Hashimoto argues the best path to massive adoption is now reusable building blocks, not polished mainline apps. Evidence: Ghostty reached one million daily macOS update checks in 18 months; libghostty reached multiple millions of daily users in 2 months. Similar trajectories cited for Pi Mono, Next.js and Tailwind.

## Key Takeaways

- Agentic "factories" produce huge quantity of software; AI is especially good at gluing proven, well-documented components, and prefers to when unprompted.
- The barrier to understanding components well enough to assemble them is gone, so imports are up.
- Exports are up too: factory artifacts targeting a hundred users can ship faster and looser than mainline apps that must weigh every feature.
- Acknowledged negatives: security vulnerabilities, instability, poor understanding of load-bearing systems.
- Even applications act as building blocks (Ghostty app has more forks with custom patches than ever).
