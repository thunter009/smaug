Multi-agent AI hedge fund orchestrator (45K+ stars per the tweet). Investor-persona agents and analysis agents feed a risk manager and portfolio manager that make trading decisions. Not financial advice.

## Key Features

- Investor agents modeled on Damodaran, Graham, Ackman, Cathie Wood, Munger, Burry, Pabrai, Lynch, Fisher, Jhunjhunwala, Druckenmiller, Buffett
- Analysis agents: valuation, sentiment, fundamentals, technicals
- Risk Manager sets risk metrics and position limits
- Portfolio Manager combines signals into the final decision
- Suggested extension: backtest decisions, keep agents that improve results, drop those that lower win rate
