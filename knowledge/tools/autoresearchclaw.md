Fully autonomous research pipeline: from one idea it reviews literature, debates hypotheses with multiple agents, writes and self-heals experiment code, and drafts a citation-verified conference paper.

## Key Features

- Searches arXiv and Semantic Scholar; verifies citations against DataCite/CrossRef
- 23-stage loop per @DataChaz; --auto-approve skips human gates
- Outputs paper draft, ICML/ICLR LaTeX, experiment scripts and results
- Extends Karpathy's autoresearch experiment loop
