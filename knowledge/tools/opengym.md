Open-source, self-hosted gym and body-weight tracker (JavaScript, ~15.7k stars). Plan weekly routines, log sets, chart body weight, see muscles trained, import from Strong/Hevy/FitNotes. Passkey login, PWA, iOS/Watch app and Android APK also exist. Workout data stays on your own server.

## Key Features

- Weekly training plans with animated exercise demos
- Body weight charts and per-muscle training view
- Import from Strong, Hevy, FitNotes
- Self-host via Docker; passkey (WebAuthn) auth; MCP topic listed
