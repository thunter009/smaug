Graph-native infrastructure for context and accountable AI systems. Turns data into knowledge graphs, records agent decisions with provenance, and lets you trace why a decision was made. MIT licensed, self-hosted, Python 3.11+. 14k+ stars.

## Key Features
- Context graphs and decision intelligence
- End-to-end provenance and traceability
- Deterministic reasoning
- Ontology management
- RDF and LPG polyglot graph storage, W3C standards
- Aimed at high-stakes, regulated domains
