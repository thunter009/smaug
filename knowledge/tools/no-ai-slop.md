# no-ai-slop

Open-source /no-ai-slop skill by Peter Yang. It edits any piece of writing to remove 20+ recurring AI-slop patterns. The author uses AI for spelling, grammar and clarity edits, and the skill strips the tells models keep producing.

## Key Features

- Removes 20+ slop patterns from prose
- Binary contrasts: "It's not X. It's Y."
- Throat-clearing openers: "Here's what nobody tells you."
- Fake-profound endings: "The future isn't coming. It's already here."
- Intended for editing a manual first draft, followed by a manual final pass, not for end-to-end AI generation
