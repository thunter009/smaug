ML video super-resolution and frame interpolation framework (C++, AGPL-3.0, ~22k stars). Free local alternative to Topaz Video AI's subscription. Started at Hack the Valley II, 2018, by K4YT3X.

## Key Features

- Upscale 480p footage to 1080p/4K with Real-ESRGAN (real-world) or Real-CUGAN (anime)
- Anime4K v4 shaders for real-time upscaling via MPV
- RIFE frame interpolation (24 fps to 60 fps)
- Vulkan GPU acceleration: NVIDIA, AMD, Intel Arc
- Windows, Linux, Docker; no watermark, no cloud
