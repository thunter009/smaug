Fake terminal for toddlers. Single HTML file, no dependencies, offline. Every keypress gets a positive, safe response: ASCII animals and vehicles, colors, letters, numbers, keyboard-smash effects.

## Key Features

- 20 animals and 12 vehicles with ASCII art
- Colors, alphabet, number visualization (count-up for 1000+)
- Keyboard-smash detection with varied responses
- Picture challenges nudge toward real words
- No scores, failures, ads, or accounts; light/dark mode
