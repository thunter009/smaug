Self-hosted AI accounting app for freelancers and small businesses. Reads receipts, invoices, and PDFs, extracts fields with an LLM, and stores them in a structured database. Early-stage per the README.

## Key Features

- AI extraction of dates, amounts, merchants, line items, and taxes from photos and PDFs
- Auto-categorization and item splitting for invoices
- Currency conversion at historical exchange rates, including crypto
- Custom fields defined with user-written AI prompts
- Choice of LLM provider: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, or a local model
- Multi-project support, filtering, import/export, custom categories
